XBO BANK — ACCOUNT OPENING GUIDE
==================================

PERSONAL ACCOUNT TYPES
-----------------------

1. Standard Checking Account
   - Minimum opening deposit: $25
   - Monthly maintenance fee: $12/month (waived with $1,500 minimum daily balance or qualifying direct deposit of $500+/month)
   - Free unlimited debit card transactions
   - Free online and mobile banking access
   - Includes complimentary first order of checks (50 checks)
   - Overdraft protection available (linked savings or credit line)

2. High-Yield Savings Account
   - Minimum opening deposit: $100
   - Annual Percentage Yield (APY): 4.15% on balances up to $250,000; 4.25% on balances above $250,000
   - No monthly maintenance fee with $300 minimum balance (otherwise $5/month)
   - Up to 6 free withdrawals per statement cycle; $3 fee per additional withdrawal
   - Interest compounded daily and credited monthly

3. Student Checking Account
   - Available for students aged 17–24 with valid student ID
   - No minimum opening deposit
   - No monthly maintenance fee for up to 5 years while enrolled
   - Free debit card with no foreign transaction fees
   - Automatic conversion to Standard Checking upon graduation or age 25

ELIGIBILITY REQUIREMENTS
-------------------------

To open an account with XBO Bank, applicants must meet the following criteria:
- Be at least 18 years of age (17 for Student Checking with parental co-signer)
- Provide a valid government-issued photo ID (driver's license, passport, or state ID)
- Provide a valid Social Security Number (SSN) or Individual Taxpayer Identification Number (ITIN)
- Supply proof of current residential address dated within the last 60 days (utility bill, lease agreement, bank statement, or government correspondence)
- Pass standard identity verification and ChexSystems screening

OPENING PROCESS
----------------

Online Application:
1. Visit www.xbobank.com/open-account
2. Select account type and provide personal information
3. Upload or photograph required documents
4. Fund your account via ACH transfer, debit card, or wire transfer
5. Account is typically approved within 1–2 business days
6. Debit card mailed within 5–7 business days; virtual card available immediately

In-Branch Application:
1. Visit any XBO Bank branch location with required documents
2. Meet with a banking associate to review account options
3. Complete and sign the account agreement
4. Make your opening deposit (cash, check, or transfer)
5. Receive a temporary debit card on the spot; permanent card mailed within 5–7 business days

JOINT ACCOUNTS
---------------

Joint accounts are available for all personal account types. Both account holders must be present (in-branch) or complete separate identity verification (online). Each holder has equal rights to the account. Joint accounts require both parties to provide valid ID, SSN, and proof of address.

BUSINESS ACCOUNTS
------------------

XBO Bank offers Business Checking and Business Savings accounts for sole proprietors, LLCs, partnerships, and corporations. Required documentation includes: EIN or SSN (sole proprietor), articles of incorporation or organization, business license, and valid photo ID for all authorized signers. Business Checking has a $25 monthly fee, waived with $5,000 average monthly balance. Contact our Business Banking team at 1-800-926-2265, option 4, for more details.

For questions about account opening, contact us at 1-800-926-2265 or visit any XBO Bank branch.
