XBO BANK — CREDIT CARD PRODUCTS
==================================

1. XBO REWARDS CARD
--------------------

The XBO Rewards Card is our most popular everyday credit card, offering simple and generous cashback on all purchases with no annual fee.

- Annual Fee: $0
- APR: 17.49% – 25.49% variable (based on creditworthiness and Prime Rate)
- Introductory APR: 0% APR on purchases and balance transfers for the first 15 months; after that, the standard variable APR applies
- Rewards Rate: 1.5% unlimited cashback on every purchase, everywhere — no categories to track, no spending caps
- Welcome Bonus: Earn $200 cashback after spending $1,000 on purchases within the first 90 days of account opening
- Credit Limit Range: $1,000 – $25,000
- Foreign Transaction Fee: 3%
- Late Payment Fee: Up to $40
- Balance Transfer Fee: 3% of the transfer amount ($10 minimum)
- Benefits:
  - Free FICO credit score updated monthly on statements and in the XBO Mobile App
  - $0 fraud liability — you pay nothing for unauthorized charges
  - Purchase protection covering new purchases against damage or theft for 90 days (up to $500 per claim, $50,000 per year)
  - Extended warranty: Extends manufacturer's warranty by up to 1 additional year on eligible items
  - Contactless tap-to-pay and digital wallet compatible (Apple Pay, Google Pay, Samsung Pay)
- Eligibility: Minimum credit score of 670 recommended; subject to credit approval

- Rewards Rate: 1% cashback on all purchases

- Late Payment Fee: Up to $35
- Benefits:
