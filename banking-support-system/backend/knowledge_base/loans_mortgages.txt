XBO BANK — LOANS & MORTGAGES
==============================

PERSONAL LOANS
---------------

XBO Bank offers unsecured personal loans ranging from $1,000 to $50,000 with fixed interest rates and no prepayment penalties.

Loan Terms: 12, 24, 36, 48, or 60 months
APR Range: 6.99% – 24.99% (rate based on creditworthiness and loan term)

Credit Score Tiers:
- Excellent (750+):     6.99% – 10.49% APR
- Good (700–749):       10.50% – 14.99% APR
- Fair (650–699):       15.00% – 19.99% APR
- Below Average (600–649): 20.00% – 24.99% APR
- Below 600:            Not eligible for unsecured personal loans; secured loan options available

Origination Fee: 1.00% – 5.00% of loan amount (deducted from disbursement)
Funding Time: Approved loans funded within 1–3 business days via direct deposit
Maximum Debt-to-Income Ratio: 43%

HOME MORTGAGES
---------------

Fixed-Rate Mortgages:
- 30-Year Fixed: Starting at 6.75% APR
- 15-Year Fixed: Starting at 5.99% APR
- 10-Year Fixed: Starting at 5.50% APR

Adjustable-Rate Mortgages (ARM):
- 5/1 ARM: Starting at 5.85% APR (fixed for 5 years, adjusts annually; 2% annual cap, 6% lifetime cap)
- 7/1 ARM: Starting at 6.10% APR (fixed for 7 years, adjusts annually; 2% annual cap, 6% lifetime cap)

Down Payment Requirements:
- Conventional Loan: Minimum 5% down (PMI required for less than 20% down)
- FHA Loan: Minimum 3.5% down (credit score 580+); 10% down (credit score 500–579)
- VA Loan: 0% down for eligible veterans and active-duty service members
- Jumbo Loan: Minimum 10% down for loan amounts exceeding $766,550

Closing Costs: Typically 2% – 5% of the purchase price, including appraisal, title insurance, attorney fees, and origination charges.

AUTO LOANS
-----------

- New Vehicle: APR starting at 5.49% for 36–72 month terms
- Used Vehicle (2019 or newer): APR starting at 6.29% for 36–72 month terms
- Maximum loan-to-value: 100% for new, 90% for used vehicles
- Maximum term: 72 months (84 months available for loans over $30,000 on new vehicles)
- No application fee; $150 processing fee at closing

HOME EQUITY LINE OF CREDIT (HELOC)
------------------------------------

- Variable APR: Prime rate + 0.50% to Prime rate + 4.50% (currently 8.50% – 12.50%)
- Draw period: 10 years (interest-only payments)
- Repayment period: 20 years (principal + interest)
- Minimum credit line: $10,000; Maximum: $500,000
- Combined loan-to-value must not exceed 85%
- No annual fee for the first year; $75/year thereafter
- $500 early closure fee if closed within 36 months of opening

APPLICATION PROCESS
--------------------

1. Pre-qualification: Check your estimated rate online without affecting your credit score (soft inquiry)
2. Full Application: Submit documentation including pay stubs (last 30 days), W-2s (last 2 years), bank statements (last 2 months), and government-issued ID
3. Underwriting: Review typically completed within 3–5 business days for personal/auto loans; 30–45 days for mortgages
4. Approval & Closing: Sign loan documents electronically or in-branch; funds disbursed per loan type timeline

For loan inquiries, call 1-800-926-2265, option 3, or schedule an appointment at any XBO Bank branch.
