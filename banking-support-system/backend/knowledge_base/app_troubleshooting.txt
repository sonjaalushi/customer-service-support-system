XBO BANK — APP & DIGITAL BANKING TROUBLESHOOTING
====================================================

MOBILE APP ISSUES
------------------

Login Errors:
- "Invalid Credentials" message: Verify that your username and password are entered correctly. Passwords are case-sensitive. If you have recently changed your password, ensure you are using the updated credentials.
- Account Locked: After 5 consecutive failed login attempts, your online banking access is temporarily locked for 30 minutes. If you cannot wait, call 1-800-926-2265, option 0, to have your account unlocked by a representative.
- "Session Expired" error: This occurs after 10 minutes of inactivity for security purposes. Log in again to resume your session.
- Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) issues: If you are not receiving your verification code via SMS, check that your phone number on file is current (Settings > Profile > Contact Information). Codes expire after 5 minutes. If SMS delivery is delayed, tap "Resend Code" or select "Call Me Instead" for a voice-delivered code. You may also use the XBO Authenticator feature within the app as an alternative.

App Crashes & Performance:
- Force close the app and reopen it. On iPhone: swipe up from the bottom and swipe the app away. On Android: tap the recent apps button and swipe the app away.
- Ensure your app is updated to the latest version (minimum supported versions: iOS 15.0+, Android 10.0+). Update via the App Store or Google Play Store.
- Clear the app cache: Go to your device Settings > Apps > XBO Mobile > Storage > Clear Cache (Android). On iPhone, uninstall and reinstall the app to clear cached data.
- Restart your device if the app continues to crash.
- If the problem persists after updating and restarting, uninstall and reinstall the app.

Biometric Login Issues (Face ID / Touch ID / Fingerprint):
- Ensure biometric login is enabled: Open XBO Mobile App > Settings > Security > Biometric Login > Toggle ON.
- If biometrics fail repeatedly, disable and re-enable the feature in app settings.
- Make sure your device biometrics are properly configured in your device's system settings.
- After a password change, biometric login must be re-enabled by logging in once with your username and password.
- Biometric login is not available on rooted or jailbroken devices for security reasons.

Mobile Check Deposit Issues:
- Ensure the check is properly endorsed: Sign the back of the check and write "For Mobile Deposit Only — XBO Bank" below your signature.
- Photo quality: Place the check on a dark, flat surface with good lighting. Ensure all four corners of the check are visible and the image is in focus. Avoid shadows and glare.
- Deposit limits: Standard daily mobile deposit limit is $5,000; monthly limit is $25,000. Premium Checking customers have a $10,000 daily limit and $50,000 monthly limit.
- "Deposit Not Accepted" error: This may occur if the check has already been deposited, the check is older than 180 days (stale-dated), or the check amount exceeds your deposit limit. Contact customer service for assistance.
- Funds availability: First $225 available immediately; remaining funds available within 1–2 business days for established accounts (accounts open 30+ days).

Balance Not Updating:
- Account balances are updated in real time for most transactions. However, pending transactions (debit card holds, recent deposits) may take 1–3 business days to fully post.
- Pull down on the account screen to manually refresh your balance.
- If your balance has not updated after 3 business days, contact customer service at 1-800-926-2265.

WEBSITE ISSUES
---------------

Forgot Password:
1. Go to www.xbobank.com/login and click "Forgot Password"
2. Enter your registered email address or username
3. Answer your security questions or receive a one-time verification code via email or SMS
4. Create a new password (requirements: minimum 10 characters, at least 1 uppercase letter, 1 lowercase letter, 1 number, and 1 special character)
5. New password takes effect immediately; you will be logged out of all other active sessions

Accessing Statements:
- Log in to online banking and navigate to Accounts > Statements & Documents
- Statements are available for the past 7 years in PDF format
- E-statements are generated by the 3rd business day of each month
- To switch from paper to e-statements: Settings > Preferences > Paperless Statements > Enroll
- Having trouble viewing PDFs? Ensure you have a compatible PDF reader. Try a different browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge recommended).

Bill Pay Issues:
- Payments scheduled before 4:00 PM ET on a business day are processed the same day
- Allow 1–2 business days for electronic payments; 5–7 business days for paper check payments
- If a payment shows as "Failed," verify the payee's account number and routing number, and ensure sufficient funds are available
- To cancel a pending payment: Bill Pay > Scheduled Payments > select payment > Cancel (cancellation must be made before the processing date)

COMMON ERROR CODES
--------------------

- Error 1001 — Authentication Failure: Your session could not be verified. Clear your browser cookies, close all browser tabs, and log in again. If the error persists, try a different browser or device.

- Error 2001 — Service Temporarily Unavailable: The requested service is currently undergoing maintenance or experiencing high traffic. Please try again in 15–30 minutes. Check www.xbobank.com/status for real-time system status.

- Error 4001 — Transaction Declined: The transaction could not be processed. Common causes include insufficient funds, exceeded daily transaction limit ($10,000 debit card, $25,000 ACH), card reported lost/stolen, or merchant category restriction. Contact 1-800-926-2265 for details.

- Error 5002 — Connection Timeout: The request timed out due to a slow or unstable network connection. Check your internet connection and try again. Switch between Wi-Fi and mobile data if needed.

- Error 6010 — Feature Not Available: This feature is not available for your account type or region. Contact customer service for more information.

SYSTEM MAINTENANCE SCHEDULE
-----------------------------

- Routine Maintenance Window: Every Sunday from 2:00 AM – 5:00 AM ET
  During this time, online banking, mobile app, and bill pay services may be intermittently unavailable. ATM services and debit card transactions remain fully operational.
- Planned Outages: Announced at least 72 hours in advance via email, in-app notification, and www.xbobank.com/status.
- Emergency Maintenance: Unscheduled outages are communicated in real time at www.xbobank.com/status and via @XBOBankHelp on Twitter/X.

For technical support, call 1-800-926-2265, option 6, or use Live Chat at www.xbobank.com (available 8:00 AM – 10:00 PM ET, Monday – Saturday).
