XBO BANK — FEES & REFUND POLICIES
=====================================

MONTHLY ACCOUNT MAINTENANCE FEES
----------------------------------

- Standard Checking: $12/month
  Waived with: $1,500 minimum daily balance OR qualifying direct deposit of $500+/month OR linked XBO savings with $5,000+ balance
- High-Yield Savings: $5/month
  Waived with: $300 minimum daily balance
- Student Checking: $0 (no monthly fee while enrolled, up to 5 years)
- Business Checking: $25/month
  Waived with: $5,000 average monthly balance
- Premium Checking: $25/month
  Waived with: $15,000 combined balance across all XBO accounts

TRANSACTION FEES
-----------------

- Overdraft Fee: $35 per item (maximum 3 overdraft fees per day; $105 daily cap)
  Note: Transactions of $5 or less will not incur an overdraft fee
- Non-Sufficient Funds (NSF) / Returned Item Fee: $35 per item
- Stop Payment Fee: $30 per request
- Domestic Wire Transfer (Outgoing): $25
- Domestic Wire Transfer (Incoming): $15
- International Wire Transfer (Outgoing): $45
- International Wire Transfer (Incoming): $15
- Cashier's Check: $10 per check (free for Premium Checking customers)
- Money Order: $5 each
- Account Research / Statement Copy: $5 per statement (older than 18 months)
- Paper Statement Fee: $3/month (free for customers aged 65+)
- Early Account Closure Fee: $25 (if closed within 90 days of opening)

ATM FEES
---------

- XBO Bank ATMs: Free and unlimited
- Non-XBO Domestic ATMs: $2.50 per transaction (first 2 per month free for Premium Checking)
- International ATMs: $5.00 per transaction plus 3% foreign exchange surcharge
- ATM owner surcharge may also apply (non-XBO ATMs)
- Balance inquiry at non-XBO ATMs: $1.00

CARD-RELATED FEES
------------------

- Debit Card Replacement: $5 (free if reported lost/stolen; free first replacement)
- Expedited Debit Card Delivery (2-day): $25
- Temporary Debit Card (in-branch): Free
- Foreign Transaction Fee (debit card): 1% of the transaction amount
- Card-Not-Present Transaction (phone/online purchases): No additional fee

REFUND POLICIES
----------------

Fee Reversal / Courtesy Waiver:
- XBO Bank offers a one-time courtesy reversal of overdraft or NSF fees per calendar year for accounts in good standing (open 6+ months, no negative balance history in last 12 months)
- To request a courtesy reversal, call 1-800-926-2265 or visit a branch
- Additional fee reversals are reviewed on a case-by-case basis by account managers

Dispute-Based Refunds:
- If you believe a fee was charged in error, submit a dispute within 60 days of the statement date
- Disputes can be filed online via XBO Mobile App > Account Services > Dispute a Fee, by phone, or in-branch
- Investigation completed within 10 business days; provisional credit issued within 3 business days if eligible
- Written notification of outcome sent via email or mail

FEE WAIVERS — SPECIAL CATEGORIES
----------------------------------

Military & Veterans:
- All monthly maintenance fees waived on personal accounts
- No foreign transaction fees on debit or credit cards
- Free cashier's checks and money orders (up to 10/month)
- Reduced rates on personal loans (0.25% APR discount)

Senior Citizens (65+):
- Monthly maintenance fee waived on Standard Checking and Savings
- Free paper statements
- Free cashier's checks (up to 5/month)
- No non-XBO ATM fees (up to 4 transactions/month)

Students:
- Student Checking: No monthly fee for up to 5 years
- No overdraft fee on the first overdraft per semester
- Free debit card replacement once per year

For questions about fees or to request a refund, contact XBO Bank at 1-800-926-2265 or visit any branch location.
